# Lesson 18: Dynamic Roadmapping & Capacity Planning in Volatile Business Climates

> **Module 3: Reinventing Agile & Delivery with AI** | *Seviye / Level: İleri / Advanced Lead*

---

### 📌 Executive Summary & Mindset Shift
Static 6-month Gantt charts are dead on arrival. Building rolling, resilient roadmaps with AI scenario stress-testing and adaptive buffer modeling.

---

### 💡 ELI5 Metaphor & Intuitive Story
> Carving a rigid 6-month sailing itinerary into stone during hurricane season is madness. An astute captain defines destinations but recalibrates waypoints constantly based on sea states and barometric pressure. Your roadmap is a live GPS, not a poured concrete monument.

---

### ⚖️ The Legacy Way vs. The AI-Driven Way
| Dimension | The Legacy Way | The AI-Driven Way |
|---|---|---|
| **Yol Haritası Yapısı** | Aylara bölünmüş katı Gantt şemaları hazırlayıp ilk gecikmede paniklemek. | Şimdi / Sonra / Gelecek (Now / Next / Later) sütunlarıyla dinamik önceliklendirme. |
| **Kapasite Tamponu** | Herkesin %100 kapasiteyle çalışacağını varsaymak (hastalık, izin, kriz yokmuş gibi). | Tarihsel volatiliteye göre %25 'Bilinmeyenler Tamponu' (Unplanned Buffer) koymak. |
| **Öncelik Değişimi** | Yönetim yeni fikirle geldiğinde 'Tüm planımız bozuldu' diye kavga etmek. | AI ile senaryo analizi yapıp: 'X'i eklersek Y 3 hafta sarkar, hangisini seçelim?' diyebilmek. |

---

### 🚀 Step-by-Step Actionable Framework
- 1. Implement Now / Next / Later: Now (Committed current cycle), Next (High-confidence pipeline), Later (Strategic architectural direction).
- 2. Enforce the Zero-Sum Rule: When execs inject new scope, don't say 'Impossible'; ask: 'Which active commitment are we trading out to make room?'
- 3. AI Scenario Stress-Testing: Feed product dependency trees into an LLM to evaluate the ripple effect of delaying or accelerating key epics.
- 4. Communicate Confidence Intervals: Never promise 'October 12th'; present 'Targeting late Q3 with 80% confidence window.'

---

### 🚨 Real-World War Story / Case Study
At a B2B SaaS startup, the CEO interrupted the engineering squad every Monday with ad-hoc enterprise feature requests, leaving 5 epics half-built. Incoming lead Gokhan implemented an AI Capacity Ripple Simulator. Next time the CEO requested an emergency customization, Gokhan presented an AI trade-off brief within 15 minutes: 'Adding this pushes the European rollout by 4 weeks, risking a $75,000 SLA penalty.' The CEO backed off, respecting engineering capacity.
