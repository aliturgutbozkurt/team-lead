# Lesson 03: The First 90 Days Survival Kit: AI Stakeholder Mapping & Rapid Trust Building

> **Module 1: The Transition: From Coder to AI-Augmented Leader** | *Seviye / Level: Başlangıç / Beginner Lead*

---

### 📌 Executive Summary & Mindset Shift
Securing early credibility without breaking production: listening tours, AI-assisted stakeholder mapping, and executing high-impact quick wins.

---

### 💡 ELI5 Metaphor & Intuitive Story
> When you buy an old house, you don't smash load-bearing walls on night one. You inspect the electrical panel, test water pressure, and see which floorboards creak. Your first 90 days are an MRI scan and trust-building phase, not a demolition derby.

---

### ⚖️ The Legacy Way vs. The AI-Driven Way
| Dimension | The Legacy Way | The AI-Driven Way |
|---|---|---|
| **İlk 30 Gün** | Hemen mimariyi baştan yazma planı açıklayıp ekibi korkutmak. | Herkesle 30 dk 1-on-1 yapıp transkriptleri AI ile analiz ederek gizli acıları bulmak. |
| **İlk 60 Gün** | Büyük vaatler verip üst yönetimi gereksiz beklentiye sokmak. | Geliştirici ortamındaki en büyük 2 sürtünmeyi (CI süresi, flaky test) çözmek. |
| **İlk 90 Gün** | Yalnız kovboy gibi her kararı tek başına almaya çalışmak. | Takımla mutabık kalınan ortak AI vizyonunu ve çeyreklik hedefleri sunmak. |

---

### 🚀 Step-by-Step Actionable Framework
- 1. 30-Day Listening Tour: Interview engineers, PMs, designers, and sibling leads: 'What single thing slows you down the most?'
- 2. AI Pain-Point Synthesis: Pass anonymized notes to an LLM to extract recurring organizational bottlenecks and cognitive blockers.
- 3. High-Leverage Quick Win: Fix something visceral—like cutting build times from 25m to 6m or killing a hated flaky test.
- 4. Deposit into the Trust Bank: Shield the squad from calendar spam; prove on day one that you protect their deep focus.

---

### 🚨 Real-World War Story / Case Study
Can joined a tier-1 banking team and decreed on day three: 'We are rewriting everything in Rust.' Senior devs revolted, leading to two pending resignations. To rescue the team, Can pivoted to structured listening. An AI synthesis of 1-on-1 notes revealed the real friction was 14 hours of weekly status theater. He slashed meetings via async AI digests, earned trust, and prevented key attrition.
