# Lesson 06: The Modern Lead's AI Stack: Cursor, Claude 3.7, Copilot, NotebookLM & Deep Research

> **Module 1: The Transition: From Coder to AI-Augmented Leader** | *Seviye / Level: Başlangıç / Beginner Lead*

---

### 📌 Executive Summary & Mindset Shift
Cutting through the tooling frenzy: selecting, budgeting, and weaving Cursor, Claude 3.7, Copilot, NotebookLM, and research agents into high-impact daily cadences.

---

### 💡 ELI5 Metaphor & Intuitive Story
> Buying every hyped AI tool is like flooding a woodworking shop with 50 specialty Japanese saws every week. Nobody builds furniture; they just argue over blade teeth. A great lead standardizes on three core instruments: the surgical IDE assistant, the strategic reasoning engine, and the organizational research memory.

---

### ⚖️ The Legacy Way vs. The AI-Driven Way
| Dimension | The Legacy Way | The AI-Driven Way |
|---|---|---|
| **IDE Entegrasyonu** | Sadece satır içi autocomplete eklentisi kullanmak. | Multi-file context ve repo indeksleme yapabilen Cursor/Copilot Workspace kullanmak. |
| **Mimari Analiz** | Saatlerce Google ve StackOverflow sayfalarında kaybolmak. | Claude 3.7 Sonnet / Reasoning modelleriyle karmaşık mimari senaryoları modellemek. |
| **Dokümantasyon & Öğrenme** | PDF ve Confluence sayfalarını kimsenin okumaması. | Tüm şirket dokümanlarını NotebookLM'e yükleyip sesli podcast ve interaktif SSS üretmek. |

---

### 🚀 Step-by-Step Actionable Framework
- 1. Tactical Layer (IDE): Multi-file indexing and surgical refactoring via Cursor or GitHub Copilot.
- 2. Strategic Reasoning Layer: Architectural trade-offs, ADR reviews, and edge-case modeling with Claude 3.7 Sonnet / o-series.
- 3. Collective Memory Layer: Slurp architecture specs, PRDs, and incident post-mortems into NotebookLM for instant audio and interactive team syntheses.
- 4. ROI Justification: Defend a $20-40/seat/month AI spend with cold metrics: 6+ engineering focus hours reclaimed per dev each week.

---

### 🚨 Real-World War Story / Case Study
A fintech squad was paying licenses for 12 disparate AI subscriptions with zero workflow consistency. Lead Elif enacted an AI consolidation: standardized on Cursor + Claude 3.7 + NotebookLM. She imported the entire architecture repo into NotebookLM, enabling new joiners to master payment flows in 48 hours. Tooling costs dropped 40% while onboarding velocity spiked.
