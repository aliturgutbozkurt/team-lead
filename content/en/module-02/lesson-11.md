# Lesson 11: Mentorship vs. Coaching: Accelerating Junior & Mid-Level Engineers 3x with AI

> **Module 2: AI-Powered People Leadership & 1-on-1s** | *Seviye / Level: Orta / Mid Lead*

---

### 📌 Executive Summary & Mindset Shift
Telling the answer (mentorship) vs. asking powerful questions (coaching). Enabling junior and mid-level devs to build custom AI pair-coaches.

---

### 💡 ELI5 Metaphor & Intuitive Story
> Give someone a fish, they eat for a day. Teach them to fish, they eat for life. Equip them with an AI sonar tracker and train their intuition, and they build their own fishing fleet.

---

### ⚖️ The Legacy Way vs. The AI-Driven Way
| Dimension | The Legacy Way | The AI-Driven Way |
|---|---|---|
| **Sorulan Soruya Cevap** | 'Git config dosyasındaki 42. satırı değiştir' deyip kestirip atmak. | 'Sence bu hata neden kaynaklanıyor olabilir? LLM ile hangi hipotezleri test ettin?' |
| **Öğrenme Hızı** | Junior'ın lead'in boş vaktini saatlerce beklemesi. | Junior'ın takım bağlamıyla eğitilmiş repo RAG botuna sorup anında öğrenmesi. |
| **Bağımsızlık** | Sürekli lead'e bağımlı, inisiyatif alamayan mühendisler. | Kendi çözümünü üretip lead'e sadece onay ve mimari doğrulama için gelen mühendisler. |

---

### 🚀 Step-by-Step Actionable Framework
- 1. Deploy the GROW Framework: Goal -> Reality -> Options -> Will (Which path will you commit to?).
- 2. Break the 'Answer Dispenser' Habit: When an engineer asks for a fix, counter with: 'What two hypotheses have you formulated so far?'
- 3. Configure Socratic AI Prompts: Guide your junior devs to set up system instructions that query their reasoning rather than vomiting boilerplate answers.
- 4. Reverse Mentorship: Have junior developers host monthly show-and-tells on novel LLM plugins and prompt hacks they discovered.

---

### 🚨 Real-World War Story / Case Study
Associate engineer Ece pinged her team lead every 20 minutes with syntax questions, exhausting her lead and eroding her own confidence. Her lead pivoted from spoon-feeding to coaching: set up a local codebase RAG tool and gave Ece a Socratic reasoning prompt. Ece bounced ideas off the model first, distilling her questions to high-level trade-offs once a day. Within 60 days, she was promoted to Mid-Level engineer.
