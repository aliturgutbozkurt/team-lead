# Lesson 07: Ditch Boring 1-on-1s: Growth-Centric, AI-Assisted One-on-One Frameworks

> **Module 2: AI-Powered People Leadership & 1-on-1s** | *Seviye / Level: Orta / Mid Lead*

---

### 📌 Executive Summary & Mindset Shift
1-on-1s are not status meetings. Build high-empathy, growth-focused cadences with AI action tracking and recurring topic synthesis.

---

### 💡 ELI5 Metaphor & Intuitive Story
> Asking 'What's the status of ticket #42?' in a 1-on-1 is like going to a cardiology check-up just to ask what time it is. Jira already shows ticket status. The 1-on-1 is sacred space to check career vitals, unearth hidden anxiety, and clear roadblocks.

---

### ⚖️ The Legacy Way vs. The AI-Driven Way
| Dimension | The Legacy Way | The AI-Driven Way |
|---|---|---|
| **Gündem Belirleme** | Toplantıya hazırlıksız girip 'Eee, ne var ne yok?' demek. | Önceki notlar, PR ritmi ve hedeflerden AI ile kişiselleştirilmiş 3 odak sorusu üretmek. |
| **İçerik** | 30 dakikanın 25 dakikasını sprint durumunu anlatarak harcamak. | Zamanın %80'ini kariyer, motivasyon, takım ilişkileri ve öğrenmeye ayırmak. |
| **Aksiyon Takibi** | Alınan kararların unutulup gitmesi. | Görüşme sonrasında AI ile 3 maddelik aksiyon e-postası çıkarıp takvime işlemek. |

---

### 🚀 Step-by-Step Actionable Framework
- 1. The 10-10-10 Protocol: 10m their agenda (frustrations, ideas), 10m your coaching agenda, 10m future career progression.
- 2. Ban Status Reports: 'If it lives on a Jira board, keep it off our 1-on-1 calendar. This time is for your growth.'
- 3. AI-Assisted Prep: Prompt an LLM with previous 1-on-1 takeaways: 'Identify recurring blockages or subtle signs of disengagement.'
- 4. The Inversion Closing: End every session with: 'What is one thing I can start or stop doing to make your work life easier this week?'

---

### 🚨 Real-World War Story / Case Study
Kerem, a talented backend dev, was mentally checked out and interviewing elsewhere. His previous lead used 1-on-1s solely to audit Jira cards. Incoming lead Seda introduced the 10-10-10 framework. Within 15 minutes, Kerem confessed he was starving for distributed systems work but trapped in legacy UI fixes. Seda realigned his roadmap; Kerem stayed and engineered the company's highest-throughput caching engine.
