# Lesson 08: Difficult Conversations & Constructive Feedback: The SBI Model + AI Roleplay

> **Module 2: AI-Powered People Leadership & 1-on-1s** | *Seviye / Level: Orta / Mid Lead*

---

### 📌 Executive Summary & Mindset Shift
Overcoming conflict avoidance: Mastering Situation-Behavior-Impact (SBI) feedback and stress-testing difficult conversations via AI roleplay.

---

### 💡 ELI5 Metaphor & Intuitive Story
> Not telling your coworker they have spinach stuck between their teeth isn't kindness—it's letting them walk around embarrassing themselves all day. Constructive feedback is quietly pointing out the spinach with empathy and zero judgment.

---

### ⚖️ The Legacy Way vs. The AI-Driven Way
| Dimension | The Legacy Way | The AI-Driven Way |
|---|---|---|
| **Geri Bildirim Tarzı** | 'Sen çok dikkatsizsin, kodların sürekli patlıyor' (Kişiliğe saldırı). | 'Dünkü release'de (Durum), test yazmadan PR açtın (Davranış), ödeme servisi 12 dk çöktü (Etki)' (SBI). |
| **Zamanlama** | 6 ay biriktirip yıllık performans değerlendirmesinde yüzüne vurmak. | Olaydan sonraki en geç 48 saat içinde sıcağı sıcağına konuşmak. |
| **Hazırlık** | Spontane konuşup öfkelenmek veya konuyu yumuşatıp mesajı kaybetmek. | Görüşme öncesi LLM ile rol yapıp adayın vereceği olası tepkileri simüle etmek. |

---

### 🚀 Step-by-Step Actionable Framework
- 1. Apply the SBI Model: Ground your feedback in observable reality: Situation -> Behavior -> Impact.
- 2. Critique the Action, Never the Person: Never utter 'You are lazy'; state 'Missing two consecutive PR reviews stalled our staging deployment.'
- 3. Pre-Game via AI Roleplay: Feed Claude the context: 'Roleplay as a defensive senior dev reacting to negative feedback. Challenge my points.'
- 4. Co-Create the Remedy: Ask: 'What mechanisms can we put in place so this friction doesn't repeat?' Make them the author of the fix.

---

### 🚨 Real-World War Story / Case Study
In an insurtech squad, a brilliant but arrogant senior engineer left caustic PR comments: 'Who wrote this garbage? A child could do better.' Lead Ozan hesitated to confront him for 3 months until an associate engineer handed in a tearful resignation. Desperate, Ozan stress-tested the confrontation using Claude roleplay, then delivered an SBI debrief. The senior realized his blind spot, apologized, and re-anchored team norms.
