# Lesson 34: Modern Engineering Career Ladders in the LLM Era

> **Module 6: AI-Era Hiring, Onboarding & Talent Strategy** | *Seviye / Level: İleri / Advanced Lead*

---

### 📌 Executive Summary & Mindset Shift
Tenure-based seniority is extinct. Rebuilding engineering career ladders based on system impact, architectural judgment, and AI leverage multipliers.

---

### 💡 ELI5 Metaphor & Intuitive Story
> You don't evaluate a master carpenter by how many years they sat on a stool. An apprentice sands a chair leg (Junior). A journeyman builds the dining table autonomously (Mid). A master craftsman designs entire interior architecture and mentors the shop (Senior). The principal engineer designs the automated timber mill (Staff/AI Lead).

---

### ⚖️ The Legacy Way vs. The AI-Driven Way
| Dimension | The Legacy Way | The AI-Driven Way |
|---|---|---|
| **Kıdem Tanımı** | '5 yıl tecrübesi olan otomatik olarak Senior olur.' | Yıl önemsizdir; problem çözme kapsamı (Scope of Impact) ve otonomluk belirler. |
| **Junior Rolü** | Basit HTML/CSS ve CRUD işleri yazan amele. | AI eşlikçileriyle tam kapsamlı modül geliştiren, öğrenme hızı yüksek mühendis. |
| **Senior / Staff Rolü** | En zor fonksiyonları tek başına karanlık odada yazan kişi. | Mimari sınırları çizen, AI ajanlarını takıma entegre eden ve iş birimleriyle strateji kuran lider. |

---

### 🚀 Step-by-Step Actionable Framework
- 1. Scope of Impact Over Years on Seat: Junior (Executes defined tasks), Mid (Owns end-to-end features), Senior (Multiplies squad architecture), Staff (Transforms org-wide systems).
- 2. AI Leverage as Core Competency: Evaluate how effectively engineers amplify their velocity, testing thoroughness, and context synthesis via modern AI tools.
- 3. Transparent Engineering Rubrics: Eliminate subjective promotion favoritism with published behavioral criteria and tangible evidence portfolios.
- 4. Enforce the Dual-Track Ladder: Great individual contributors shouldn't be forced into people management; protect the Staff+ Technical Track.

---

### 🚨 Real-World War Story / Case Study
A 2-year junior engineer mastered LLM workflows and architected higher-throughput distributed modules than an 8-year legacy senior who refused modern tools. Company policy blocked promotions: 'Minimum 5 years for Senior title.' The junior was fielding external offers. Lead Tolga rewrote the company's career ladder around measurable system scope and AI leverage. The junior was promoted; high-performing talent retention soared.
