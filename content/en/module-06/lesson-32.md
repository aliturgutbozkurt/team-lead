# Lesson 32: Designing AI-Resilient Hiring Challenges & Pair-Prompting

> **Module 6: AI-Era Hiring, Onboarding & Talent Strategy** | *Seviye / Level: İleri / Advanced Lead*

---

### 📌 Executive Summary & Mindset Shift
Eliminating fraudulent take-home assignments where candidates copy-paste ChatGPT: Interactive timeboxed challenges and live pair-prompting sessions.

---

### 💡 ELI5 Metaphor & Intuitive Story
> Giving a pastry chef a homework assignment: 'Bake a cake at home' means they can buy a cake at a gourmet bakery and claim they made it. Inviting them into your kitchen for 30 minutes to make a souffle using the smart oven reveals their true mastery instantly.

---

### ⚖️ The Legacy Way vs. The AI-Driven Way
| Dimension | The Legacy Way | The AI-Driven Way |
|---|---|---|
| **Take-Home Formatı** | Adaya 3 günlük devasa ödev verip bedava amelelik yaptırmak. | Maksimum 2 saatlik, sınırları net ve AI kullanımının serbest olduğu odaklı bir problem. |
| **Değerlendirme** | Kodun çalışıp çalışmadığına bakmak. | Adayın git commit geçmişine, AI ile konuşma loglarına ve aldığı mimari kararlara bakmak. |
| **Canlı Eşleşme** | Adayı kamerada tek başına terletip sessizce izlemek. | Birlikte bir AI ajanını yönlendirerek (Pair-Prompting) problem çözmek. |

---

### 🚀 Step-by-Step Actionable Framework
- 1. The 2-Hour Ceiling: Never assign grueling 20-hour weekend take-home projects; top-tier senior engineers will simply decline your process.
- 2. The Prompt Changelog: Require candidates to submit their prompt history: evaluate how they steered the model when it hallucinated.
- 3. Live Pair-Prompting: Sit together and drive an AI assistant collaboratively; observe how the candidate crafts context and verifies outputs.
- 4. The 15-Minute Architecture Defense: Ask: 'Why did you reject the LLM's first suggestion? What were the hidden performance trade-offs?'

---

### 🚨 Real-World War Story / Case Study
An AI scaleup used a 3-day take-home test. 18 of 20 submissions were identical, boilerplate Claude outputs; separating elite talent from posers was impossible. Lead Onur replaced it with a 1-hour live Pair-Prompting session on Zoom. Together, they tackled an active WebSocket edge-case in Cursor. Observing how candidates steered context, challenged buggy LLM suggestions, and verified edge-cases made hiring accuracy skyrocket.
