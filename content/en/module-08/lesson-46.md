# Lesson 46: Negotiating Deadlines & Scope Creep with Product: The Iron Triangle

> **Module 8: Managing Up & Business Alignment** | *Seviye / Level: Yönetici / Director Level*

---

### 📌 Executive Summary & Mindset Shift
When a PM demands 'we must launch by Friday': Escaping the binary trap of toxic over-promising or combative rejection using the Iron Triangle negotiation framework.

---

### 💡 ELI5 Metaphor & Intuitive Story
> A diner ordering a slow-roasted lamb shank and demanding it served in 5 minutes. The chef can't serve raw meat (compromising quality). Saying 'impossible, get out' creates a brawl. The master chef negotiates: 'A tender shank takes 40 minutes; but if you need sustenance right now, I can serve our award-winning hot soup in 3 minutes.'

---

### ⚖️ The Legacy Way vs. The AI-Driven Way
| Dimension | The Legacy Way | The AI-Driven Way |
|---|---|---|
| **Tepki Biçimi** | Korkudan 'Yetiştiririz' deyip takımı hafta sonu dahil sabahlara kadar çalıştırmak. | 'Demir Üçgen' kuralını masaya koyup tarih sabitse neleri kapsamdan çıkaracağımızı sormak. |
| **Kalite Tavizi** | Yetişsin diye test yazmamak, dokümantasyonu silmek (Teknik borç patlaması). | Kaliteden asla taviz vermemek; sadece işlevsellik kapsamını (scope) küçültmek. |
| **İlişki Dinamiği** | Yazılımcılar ve PM'in iki düşman kampa bölünmesi. | Aynı gemideki iki stratejik ortak olarak AI ile hızlı prototip (MVP) seçenekleri üretmek. |

---

### 🚀 Step-by-Step Actionable Framework
- 1. Master the Iron Triangle: Scope, Time, and Quality/Cost. Quality is non-negotiable. If time is locked, scope must yield.
- 2. Deploy 'Yes, If...': Banish defensive rejections; say 'Yes, we can launch by Friday, if we defer the multi-currency export to phase two.'
- 3. Thin Vertical Slicing: Carve features down to the Pareto core: the 20% of engineering effort that unlocks 80% of immediate customer utility.
- 4. AI Scope Decoupling: Feed feature specs to Claude: 'Identify the absolute minimum viable slice to meet Friday's demo without structural tech debt.'

---

### 🚨 Real-World War Story / Case Study
A fintech PM demanded a 4-week Crypto Wallet module ship in 5 days for an emergency venture investor demo. The previous lead had rage-quit. Lead Ekin stepped in: 'Does the investor need to see 50 crypto tokens, or do they need to see seamless wallet deposit and withdrawal?' The PM conceded: 'Just the deposit flow.' Ekin scoped it to a single token (USDT). Shipped flawlessly Thursday afternoon; the startup secured their $2M funding round.
